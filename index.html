    About the Program

    UGNAY-SUBAY

    An AI-assisted monitoring platform for locally funded infrastructure: projects under the Local Government Support Fund (LGSF) and the 20% Development Fund. It links (ugnay) and tracks (subay) four streams of evidence on each project, so reviewers see where the sources agree, where they conflict, and what to check first.

    How it works

    1
    Collect

    The four evidence streams are brought together for every project.

    2
    Compare

    AI agents check where the sources agree or conflict and rank what to review.

    3
    Challenge

    A Red-Team Agent removes unsupported claims, bias and privacy risks.

    4
    Decide

    A person approves, returns or escalates each brief.

    5
    Report

    Findings feed the Regional and National M&E Reports for management.

    Prototype for PP5538 AI for Public Policy, Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy, NUS. Not an official DILG system.

    Limitations

    • Data coverage. Project details come from the LGSF database extract (as of 4 Nov 2026) and the LIME-20 export of 20% Development Fund projects, public fields only. SubayBAYAN project exports and LIME-20 / RPMES forms (FY 2022–2026) add geotagged-photo counts and monthly accomplishment reports; monthly RPMES reports are available mainly for projects enrolled from FY 2025. TPM findings cover the regions that have submitted terminal reports.
    • Approximate locations. Pins use barangay or town centres. Exact sites come only from geotagged photos.
    • Evidence, not verdicts. A mismatch is a reason to check, never proof of fraud. TPM ratings are perception surveys, not engineering verification. Matches to TPM reports are likely, not certain.
    • Official status untouched. AI Review Priority is separate from the official Projects-at-Risk list and never changes it.
    • Privacy. No names of citizens or respondents. Tickets and photos stay on this device in the prototype; nothing is sent anywhere.
    • Public version. RPMES risk ratings, exception reports and TPM findings are excluded; project details are limited to fields SubayBAYAN already publishes.
    • Prototype scope. Agents run fixed rules in the browser (demo mode). Six training cases are synthetic, for testing the red-team step. A live version needs a data-sharing agreement with DILG.
